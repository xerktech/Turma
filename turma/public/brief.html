One brief per org, compiled by the hub every few hours (and on Brief now) from what it already knows — the board's tickets, each session's attention state, the hub's ticket queue and the subscription limits. Needs you is every session waiting on you, oldest first; Waiting is sessions waiting on time or CI; Starts next is the hub's queue then the auto-start order, with why each is next. The phone gets one push per brief, only when the set of sessions needing you changed.
