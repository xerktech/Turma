Daily brief Meetings
An agent host of each org writes its daily brief a few minutes before the org's meeting, from your own tickets in that org: what moved since the last meeting, the week so far and what is next. Times on this page are in your time zone.
When Tenir captures a meeting, an agent host of the org reads its captions and proposes work items. Nothing reaches the board until you approve one: Approve creates the ticket on that org's board, a decision is added to the org's decisions log, and Reject sets it aside.
